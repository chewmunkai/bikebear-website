Our story

We built Talous to run our own clients' ads. Then we set it free.

It was never a side project. It was the engine that let a small Malaysian team punch far above its weight — and we realised the business owners deserved it directly.

Talous started inside a real marketing agency in Malaysia. We were running ads for 22 clients — skincare brands, clinics, F&B — and drowning in the same loop every agency drowns in: check which ads are leaking, pause the weak ones, push budget to the winners, repeat. Every day. For every account.

That manual loop was the ceiling. It capped how many clients we could take on, and it ate the evenings of our best people. So we built software to do it: an engine that audits every live ad, grades it, decides what to do with a confidence score and a written reason, and either acts or asks. We called it the command deck.

It worked so well that the question changed — from "how do we run more accounts?" to "why are we keeping this to ourselves?"

The uncomfortable truth about agencies

The business owners who pay agencies RM8,000–20,000 a month aren't paying for genius. They're paying for someone to do the daily loop and to feel like their money is in safe hands. But they rarely get to see the work — just a monthly report full of acronyms and an invoice.

Meanwhile the people who could build this themselves — the AI-savvy operators — don't need us. So we made a decision: Talous wouldn't be a tool we sell to agencies. Talous would be the agency — an AI you hire directly, for a fraction of the price, that shows you everything it does.

Why Malaysia first

Because that's where we are, and where the product was proven. Ringgit billing, WhatsApp-first communication, local businesses we actually understand. We'd rather be genuinely great for Malaysian SMEs than vaguely global. Southeast Asia is next, then the world — but in that order, and on purpose.

What we believe

Trust is earned by showing your work. An AI handling someone's ad budget should explain every move, in plain language, with its confidence on the line. No black boxes. Results over jargon. If you can't repeat the number to a friend, it doesn't count. And the owner stays in charge — Talous is as hands-off or hands-on as you want it to be.

Talous is Finnish for the health of a household's economy. That's the job: keep the economy of your business healthy — and let you sleep at night.
