How it works

An AI media buyer that shows its working.

No mystery, no black box. Here's exactly what Talous does with your ads every day — and where you stay in control.

The daily loop

Five steps, every day, on every ad you run.

It's the same loop a great media buyer follows — Talous just never sleeps, never forgets, and writes everything down.

Sync

Pulls fresh numbers straight from your Meta ad account — spend, sales, clicks, the lot.

Audit

Grades each ad against the targets you set, then drafts what it would do about it.

Memory

Folds in what's worked for your brand before, plus any notes you've left it.

Guard

Reaches a verdict with a confidence score and a plain-English reason — and a risk check.

Execute

Acts when it's confident enough and you've pre-approved that move — otherwise it asks.

The audit

Every ad gets one of five honest grades.

Winner

Scale it

Beating your targets comfortably. Talous gives it more budget so it can do more.

Healthy

Leave it

Doing its job. No change needed — and no fiddling for the sake of looking busy.

Warning

Watch it

Slipping, or something upstream (like your landing page) is leaking. Flagged for a closer look.

Kill

Stop the bleed

Spending with little to show. Talous recommends pausing before it wastes more.

No data

Give it time

Too new to judge. Talous waits for enough signal before making any call.

146 ads, graded every day. That's how a small team can confidently run the ad load of a much bigger one.

Your controls

You decide how much freedom it gets.

Talous asks before it acts

Every recommendation lands in your approval queue. Tap approve or reject. Nothing happens to your money without your say-so.

Minimum confidence to act82%

  • Set the bar, brand by brand

    Choose which moves it can make on its own — pause, kill, boost, reallocate — and how confident it must be first.

  • A safety brake that never sleeps

    If it spots something broken — like a dead tracking pixel — it halts spending and tells you. It won't fly blind.

  • It learns your brand over time

    Talous keeps a memory per brand — what worked, what you told it — and feeds it into the next cycle, getting sharper weekly.
